| 2026-11-17 | The shadow trial's setting for the coordinator's review is a continuity cost of £10 per visit by a carer unfamiliar to the client (`continuity_penalty_pence` 1000), measured against the previous 4 weeks. Other trial settings: visit window ±30 min, move cost 15p per minute, day 06:30-22:30, weekly maximum 60 h. Full runs and results: [`SHADOW_TRIAL.md`](SHADOW_TRIAL.md) | At the default cost (60p) the engine's continuity was below the coordinator's actual week (0.277 vs 0.349 overall; Leeds 0.354 vs 0.555). At £10 it beat it (0.564; Leeds 0.711 vs 0.555; Dorset 0.478 vs 0.231) and still cut travel time 17% and miles 22%. Trade-off: about 12 h more travel and 200 more miles than at the default cost, and less even hours (stdev 7.2 h vs 5.8 h at the default), for much better continuity. Continuity is still a tie-breaker in principle, but the trial showed a low cost lets the engine break existing carer-client relationships. One week only, so to be revisited after the coordinator's review | Decided |
| 2026-11-16 | Shadow-mode trial on the real week 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-22, with no writes to CareLineLive. Each carer gets a broad working window (placeholder 07:00-22:00) and a placeholder weekly maximum, constrained to their teams, with leave from the API. The result is compared with the coordinator's actual allocation on hours per carer, miles and travel time (same travel model for both plans), continuity and unfilled visits. The coordinator reviews it (Decision 2 in PLAN.md) | Consequence of the two decisions above: real working hours are not entered yet, so placeholders let the engine run on real data now. Trade-off: placeholder windows and maximums make the comparison approximate (the engine may use carers at times they would not work), but it shows what the engine does on real visits without waiting for data entry or touching live bookings | Decided |
| 2026-11-16 | The engine's hours goal is to balance carers' hours equally within each team (Leeds; Dorchester/Weymouth). It is a weighted preference, not a hard rule (travel and continuity still count, so it will not add long trips just to move an hour); a per-carer weekly maximum is the hard limit. Carers who cover several teams can even things out across them. Rest and working-time limits stay as engine rules and must be confirmed with Loidis's HR adviser before being relied on | Loidis contracts do not record hours we can read, and 8 carers are "paid hours only", so a contracted share (the engine's current wording) cannot be calculated. Trade-off: equal hours vs contracted share (equal hours ignore that some carers want fewer hours, which the weekly maximum partly covers), and fairness vs travel (a heavier balance weight means more miles) | Decided |
